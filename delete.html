Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your ATRION account and associated data

This page explains how to request deletion of your ATRION account (developer: ATRION) and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

To request that your account and associated data be deleted, follow these steps:

  • Step 1: From the email address registered to your ATRION account, send an email to theyashwaghela@gmail.com.
  • Step 2: Use the subject line “Delete my ATRION account”.
  • Step 3: In the message, state whether you want your entire account deleted or only specific data deleted (without deleting your account).
  • Step 4: We verify the request against your registered email and process it within 30 days. We will email you to confirm once it is complete.

What data is deleted

When you request account deletion, we permanently delete:

  • Your account profile and login credentials (email, password hash)
  • Your linked brokerage/trading account identifiers and stored portfolio, positions, and watchlist data
  • Any voice-command recordings processed by the ARIA assistant
  • App usage, interaction, and device/diagnostic data tied to your account

What data may be kept, and for how long

We may retain a limited set of records where required by law or for legitimate business needs (for example, transaction and financial records, and fraud-prevention or security logs). Such records are retained only as long as legally required — typically up to 7 years for financial/transaction records — after which they are deleted. These records are not used to re-identify you for marketing.
