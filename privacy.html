Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ATRION ("ATRION", "we", "us") handles information when you use the ATRION mobile and desktop applications and related services (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: the email address and credentials you use to sign in to ATRION.
  • Brokerage / trading account data: when you link a broker or trading account, we process account identifiers, balances, positions, orders, and market data needed to display your portfolio and provide analysis. ATRION does not take custody of your funds.
  • Voice input: if you use the ARIA voice assistant, audio is captured only while the microphone is active to process your spoken commands. Voice is used to fulfil your request and is not sold.
  • Usage & device data: app version, device type, log/diagnostic data, and interaction events used to operate and improve the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core functionality: authentication, portfolio tracking, charts, AI signals, and order/trade features you initiate.
  • To operate the ARIA assistant and respond to your commands.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical issues.
  • To improve features and performance.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers that host our backend and deliver the service (e.g. cloud hosting, market-data providers), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Brokers / exchanges you choose to connect, to execute the actions you request.
  • Legal authorities where required by law.

4. Data Security

Information is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS. Credentials are stored using hashing/encryption. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and usage data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion (see below).

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may close your account at any time by contacting us. To delete your account and associated data, email theyashwaghela@gmail.com.

7. Children

ATRION is intended for users 18 years and older and is not directed to children.

8. No Financial Advice

ATRION provides tools, data, and AI-generated analysis for informational purposes only. It is not financial, investment, or trading advice. Trading involves substantial risk of loss. You are solely responsible for your decisions.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.
